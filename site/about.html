About LineSmart

LineSmart is a free website with sports odds, player props, matchups and betting tools. It isn't a sportsbook: it does not take bets or hold money.

What LineSmart does

  • Odds and lines: spreads, moneylines and totals for upcoming games across major US and international leagues, with line movement since the open.
  • Odds comparison: Hard Rock Bet, DraftKings and FanDuel prices side by side, with the best available price highlighted, plus player prop lines and prices from all three for NFL and NBA.
  • Player props: prop lines with each player's recent games against the line.
  • Matchups: team form, home and away splits, injuries, season stats and head-to-head comparisons.
  • Game-day weather for outdoor games.
  • My Bets: a private bet tracker that stays in your browser on your device, or syncs between your devices if you sign in with an optional account.
  • Value finder: prices that pay more than the market's no-vig fair odds.
  • Game, player and team pages: one page per game with lines across books, line movement, weather, props and form, and final scores with closing lines afterward. Start at a league: NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college football, college basketball or UFC.
  • Line alerts: one email when a line moves or hits your number (needs the optional account).

Who runs it

LineSmart is an independent site, reachable at contact@linesmart.app. It is not owned by, and does not own, any sportsbook, and it isn't affiliated with any league or team.

Where the data comes from

  • ESPN public endpoints: schedules, scores, team and player stats, injuries, and DraftKings odds and player props as published by ESPN.
  • Hard Rock Bet, FanDuel and DraftKings odds and player props via The Odds API.
  • Weather by Open-Meteo.

Data can be delayed or wrong. Always confirm odds with the sportsbook before you bet.

How LineSmart makes money

LineSmart is free to use. It plans to earn money through affiliate links to licensed sportsbooks: if you sign up through one, LineSmart may earn a commission at no cost to you. Affiliate links are labeled and never change the odds we show. Today there are no affiliate links. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

We link to the official sites of Hard Rock Bet, DraftKings, FanDuel and BetMGM, and each link appears only in states where that sportsbook runs a legal online sportsbook. In Florida, that means Hard Rock Bet only. Listing a sportsbook doesn't mean we show its odds: odds comparison covers Hard Rock Bet, DraftKings and FanDuel (not BetMGM). Books that aren't legal online in your state are dimmed and never linked. Offers are state-specific, so what you see depends on your state.

Legal sportsbooks only

LineSmart only lists state-licensed, legal US sportsbooks. We never list, link to or promote offshore or unlicensed sportsbooks.

Responsible gambling

LineSmart is for adults 21 and older. Only bet where it's legal, and only what you can afford to lose. Gambling problem? Call or text 1-800-GAMBLER. In Florida, call or text 1-833-PLAYWISE, or the Florida Council on Compulsive Gambling at 1-888-ADMIT-IT. More help and tips: Responsible Gambling.
