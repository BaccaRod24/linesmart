Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

LineSmart is a free website with sports odds, props, matchups and betting tools. It has no ads, and you don't need to sign up to use it. Accounts are optional: you only need one if you want My Bets to sync between your devices. This policy explains what information is involved when you use it.

Summary

  • We never ask for your name. If you choose to create an optional account, we store your email address and your tracked bets with our provider Supabase (US) so your bets sync between devices. We never sell or rent your data.
  • We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless, to count page views in aggregate.
  • Your browser asks a geolocation service for your approximate state, so we only show sportsbooks that are legal there. You can set your state by hand instead.
  • If you sign up for the free weekly email, we store your email address with Supabase and send the email through Resend (US). Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Without an account, your bet tracker and preferences stay in your browser on your device.
  • LineSmart sets no cookies today. Sportsbook sites you visit from our links have their own policies.

Analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

On linesmart.app we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall traffic. It does not use cookies or other storage on your device, does not fingerprint you, and does not track you across other websites. Your browser loads a small script from static.cloudflareinsights.com and sends a page-view report to cloudflareinsights.com. We see only aggregate reports: page views and visits, the referring site, device type, browser and operating system, country, and page-load performance. Cloudflare processes this data under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. We don't use the data for advertising, and we don't sell it.

Approximate location (state-aware offers)

Sports betting is legal only in some states, and each sportsbook runs only in certain states. To show only sportsbooks that are legal where you are, your browser sends a request to ipdata.info, a free IP geolocation service. ipdata.info sees your IP address and returns your approximate country and state. Under its privacy policy, it keeps IP addresses only briefly for rate limiting.

We keep only the country and state code, in your browser (localStorage, ls-geo), for up to 24 hours. It is never sent to us. If the lookup fails, we show no sportsbook offers until you pick a state. You can choose your state with the Your state picker (stored as ls-state); that choice replaces the lookup. IP location is approximate. Sportsbooks verify your location themselves before you can bet.

Optional accounts (bet sync)

You can use LineSmart without an account. If you sign in on the Account tab, here is what happens:

  • What we store: your email address, and the bets you track in My Bets, along with the time each one was last changed. That's all. We don't ask for your name, a password or any sportsbook login.
  • Where it's stored: with our provider Supabase, on servers in the United States. Access rules on the database let each signed-in person read and change only their own bets.
  • Sign-in emails: to sign in, you enter your email and we send you a one-time sign-in code (or link) by email. Supabase sends these emails for us. There are no passwords.
  • On your device: your sign-in session is kept in your browser (localStorage, ls-sb-auth), along with the email you last used to sign in (ls-acct-email), sync status (ls-sync-v1) and a short list of deleted bets waiting to sync (ls-bets-tomb-v1). The sign-in software is loaded only when you open the Account tab or are signed in.
  • Why: only to keep your bets the same on your phone, computer and other devices. We never sell your data, use it for advertising or share it with sportsbooks.
  • Signing out keeps a copy of your bets on that device.
  • Deleting your data: the Delete my account and bets button on the Account tab deletes every bet stored in your account right away. To delete the account itself (your email address), email contact@linesmart.app from the address you sign in with, and we'll delete it and confirm.

Weekly email (newsletter)

The LineSmart weekly email is free and optional. It's sent on Thursday mornings with the week's best lines across Hard Rock Bet, DraftKings and FanDuel, hot player props and notable line moves, plus occasional notes about LineSmart features. It never contains promo codes or affiliate links.

  • Signing up: you enter your email address and confirm you're 21 or older. We then email you a confirmation link, and you're only added once you click it (double opt-in). Unconfirmed signups are deleted after 30 days.
  • What we store: your email address, whether it's pending, confirmed or unsubscribed, when those changes happened, which signup box you used, and two random codes for the confirm and unsubscribe links. To stop abuse we keep a one-way hash of the IP address of each signup attempt for up to 2 days; we never store the IP address itself.
  • Who handles it: the list is stored with Supabase in the United States, and the emails are sent by Resend in the United States as our service providers. Resend may record whether an email was delivered or bounced. We don't add tracking pixels or tracked links, and we never sell, rent or share the list.
  • On your device: after you sign up, your browser remembers the address you entered and whether you've confirmed (localStorage, ls-news-v1), so the signup box can remind you to check your inbox.
  • Unsubscribing: click Unsubscribe at the bottom of any LineSmart email. One click removes you right away, with no login. You can also email contact@linesmart.app with the subject "unsubscribe". To have your address deleted entirely rather than just unsubscribed, email us from that address and we'll delete it and confirm.

Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical data when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. It uses this data for security and to run the service. We don't receive these logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Data stored on your device

These items are saved in your browser so the site works. They stay on your device and are never sent to us, except your bets if you choose to sign in and sync (see Optional accounts):

  • Bet tracker (localStorage, ls-bets-v1 and ls-bets-prefs): bets you log in My Bets and your tracker settings. Without an account they aren't uploaded or synced anywhere. They're never linked to any sportsbook account. Use the tracker's export to keep a backup.
  • Your state (localStorage, ls-state, and the cached lookup ls-geo): see above.
  • Recently viewed (localStorage, ls-recent): up to 8 teams or players you opened, shown on the search screen.
  • Weekly email signup (localStorage, ls-news-v1): see Weekly email.
  • 21+ notice (localStorage, ls-age-ok): the time you dismissed the age notice, so it isn't shown again.
  • View preferences (sessionStorage, filter-<league>): your "Upcoming" or "Incl. today's finals" choice on a league page. It is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Offline cache (service worker and Cache Storage): copies of the site's files, the odds and stats data files, and recently opened ESPN team and player data, so the site loads fast and works offline.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Third-party requests (ESPN)

Some content loads directly from ESPN. Your browser requests team logos and player photos from ESPN's image servers (such as a.espncdn.com). Team and player pages, search results and bet grading load live data from site.web.api.espn.com. ESPN therefore receives standard request data, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you came from. ESPN's handling of that data is covered by the Disney/ESPN privacy policy.

Odds, schedule and weather files are built ahead of time on GitHub's servers from ESPN, The Odds API and Open-Meteo. Your browser does not contact The Odds API or Open-Meteo.

Sportsbook and affiliate links

LineSmart shows links to licensed sportsbooks, only in states where they're legal. Today these links go to each sportsbook's official website and are not affiliate links. We plan to add affiliate tracking links later. When we do, an affiliate link may carry a tracking code in the web address. The sportsbook or its affiliate network may then set cookies or use similar technology on their site to credit LineSmart for your sign-up. LineSmart itself sets no cookies, now or for affiliate links, and we will not receive your name, account or betting details from a sportsbook. Affiliate links will be labeled, and this page and our Affiliate Disclosure will be updated before they go live.

Other links go to helplines and other sites. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Cookies

LineSmart does not set cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless. Sportsbook sites you visit from our links may set their own cookies, as described above.

Children

LineSmart is meant for adults 21 and older and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.

Selling or sharing data

We never sell, rent or share personal information. We don't collect names. We store an email address and tracked bets only if you create an optional account, and only to sync your bets, and an email address if you sign up for the weekly email, only to send it. Supabase and Resend handle these for us as our service providers. The other services named above (Cloudflare, ipdata.info, GitHub and ESPN) receive standard request data only because your browser contacts them.
