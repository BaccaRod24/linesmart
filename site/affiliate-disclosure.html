Affiliate Disclosure

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Right now, LineSmart has no affiliate relationships. We don't earn money from any sportsbook, and no links on the site are affiliate links. In states where a sportsbook is legal, we link to its official website; those links don't pay us and carry no tracking code. The sportsbooks we link to are Hard Rock Bet, DraftKings, FanDuel and BetMGM.

LineSmart plans to make money through affiliate links to licensed sportsbooks. This page will be updated, and the links labeled, before any go live.

If that changes

We may later join affiliate programs with licensed sportsbooks. When we do:

  • LineSmart may earn a commission when you sign up or deposit through one of our links, at no extra cost to you.
  • Affiliate links will be labeled, with a short disclosure next to them.
  • Commissions won't change the odds shown. Odds come from the data sources named on each page, and the best price is highlighted whichever sportsbook offers it.
  • We'll list our affiliate partners on this page and update the effective date.
  • An affiliate link may carry a tracking code, and the sportsbook or its affiliate network may set cookies on their own site to credit the referral. LineSmart sets no cookies. See our Privacy Policy.

Our standards

  • Affiliate links will only go to sportsbooks licensed where they operate, for users 21+, and only in states where that sportsbook is legal online.
  • Offers are state-specific. Which sportsbooks and offers you see depends on your state, and some states have none.
  • No offshore sportsbooks. LineSmart only lists state-licensed, legal US sportsbooks. We never list, link to or promote offshore or unlicensed sportsbooks.
  • We won't advertise bonus amounts or promotions that the sportsbook hasn't approved and currently offers.
  • Responsible gambling information and helplines will stay on every page. See Responsible Gambling.
